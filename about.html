About LuharSensi

Simple gaming tools designed to help players find, test and improve their settings.

What is LuharSensi?

LuharSensi is a gaming-focused website created to provide useful tools and information for players. Our goal is to make finding suitable sensitivity settings and gaming resources simple and easy.

Our Goal

We want to build simple, fast and useful gaming tools that players can access from both phones and computers without complicated setup.

What We Provide

  • Gaming sensitivity tools
  • Gaming-related guides and information
  • Useful online tools
  • Simple and mobile-friendly experiences

Our Approach

LuharSensi is continuously being improved. We listen to feedback and work on making our tools faster, easier to use, and more useful for the gaming community.
